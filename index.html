Privacy Policy

PDF Genie

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PDF Genie mobile application ("the App") handles information. We respect your privacy and designed the App to work entirely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PDF Genie does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We do not require an account, login, or registration.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The text you type, the images you select, and the PDF files you create are processed and saved only on your device. The App keeps a small local database that lists the names, sizes, and dates of the PDF files you created so you can find them again. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the PDF in the App or uninstall the App.

3. Permissions

  • Photos / Gallery access: Used only when you choose to add images to a PDF. Images you pick are used locally and are not uploaded anywhere.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or phone identifiers.

4. Internet, Advertising and Third Parties

The App works fully offline. It does not use any online API, analytics service, advertising network, or third-party tracking tool.

5. Sharing Files

If you choose to share or print a PDF, the App hands the file to the sharing or printing option you select on your device. What happens after that is governed by the app or service you chose.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Data Security

Because your files stay on your device, their security depends on your device's own protection (screen lock, encryption, backups). Please keep your device secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
